# Code Of Conduct

SigmaLite is intended to be a respectful, practical engineering community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Assume good intent, but prefer concrete evidence over vague claims.
- Keep discussions focused on the code, product, documentation, or operational
  impact.
- Credit others' work.
- Respect maintainers' time by providing reproducible details.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated low-effort, off-topic, or disruptive comments.
- Pressuring maintainers to disclose or ignore security issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block users; or
decline contributions that violate this code of conduct.

If you need to report conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through a
private channel. Include links, screenshots, or context where possible.
