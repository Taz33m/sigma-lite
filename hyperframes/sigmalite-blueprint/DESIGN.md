# SigmaLite Blueprint Video Design

## Style Prompt

Excel meets architectural blueprint: a spreadsheet workspace drafted as a living engineering plan. The frame feels like a precision drafting table where CSV rows, formula measurements, chart elevations, comments, and system components are plotted into place. The tone is cinematic, technical, and grounded.

## Colors

- Blueprint navy: `#102A43`
- Drafting ink: `#071923`
- Paper white: `#F4F7FB`
- Drafting blue: `#1E88E5`
- Cyan construction line: `#67D5FF`
- Redline annotation: `#E23D3D`
- Measurement yellow: `#F6C945`
- Verification green: `#22C55E`

## Typography

- Display: `IBM Plex Sans Condensed`, heavy weights for headlines and stamps.
- Data and formulas: `IBM Plex Mono`, tabular numerals for cells, metrics, formulas, and system labels.
- Small labels use uppercase tracking; major phrases use condensed display type.

## Motion Rules

- Elements draw, stamp, calibrate, align, or unfold like drafting marks.
- Grid lines animate with transform scale, not layout properties.
- Formula and chart motion should feel measured and plotted.
- Redline comments should have a hand-marked but controlled overshoot.
- Transitions are blueprint sheet wipes, cell iris reveals, and plotter-line reveals.

## What NOT To Do

- No generic SaaS card carousel.
- No purple/cyan AI-gradient background.
- No decorative floating orbs or abstract bokeh.
- No presentation-slide layouts.
- No random motion; all motion is deterministic and measured.
